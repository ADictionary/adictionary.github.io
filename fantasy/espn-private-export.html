Private ESPN league export

Create a local JSON file containing your ESPN league's teams, rosters, standings, full matchup schedule and one selected week's player box scores.

1. Add the exporter to your bookmarks

Drag the green link below onto your browser's bookmarks bar. In Chrome, show the bar with ⌘ + Shift + B. If dragging is blocked, create a new bookmark and paste the link address as its URL.

Export private ESPN league

If bookmark dragging does not work

Right-click the green link and choose Copy Link Address. Create a new bookmark manually, paste that address into its URL/location field, and save it.

2. Export while signed in to ESPN

1
Open ESPN Fantasy Football in the same browser and sign in normally. Navigate to your private league page.
2
Click the Export private ESPN league bookmark.
3
Confirm the season and scoring week. The browser downloads a JSON file.
4
Open Fourth & Goal → Fantasy → Import ESPN export and select that file.
This is a read-only export. Your browser sends its existing ESPN session to ESPN automatically; the bookmarklet never reads, displays, stores or transmits your password or cookie values. The downloaded JSON stays on your computer until you choose it for import.

3. Privacy and compatibility

ESPN's Fantasy API is unofficial and can change. If ESPN blocks the request or your session expires, refresh your ESPN league page, sign in normally and try again. Never paste session cookies or private authentication tokens into a website or chat.

The JSON includes private team names, owners, rosters, matchup scores and player statistics. Treat it as private league data. Fourth & Goal imports it locally into this browser; it is not uploaded to a server.

Fourth & Goal · The exporter does not change ESPN rosters, lineups, waivers, trades or league settings.
